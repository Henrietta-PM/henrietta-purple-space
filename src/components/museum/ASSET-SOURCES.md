# Museum reference and assets

The museum is an original architectural interpretation, not a replica or affiliated experience.

- Architectural reference: Louisiana Museum architecture and history, https://louisiana.dk/en/museum/architecture-and-history/
- Online site-plan reference inspected during development: https://roark.archi/i/site-louisiana-museum-of-modern-art. The figure-ground drawing is credited to OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL. It is not embedded or redistributed in the app, nor used as measured construction documentation.
- Stone, grass and oak surfaces: Poly Haven `stone_tiles_02`, `grass_ground`, `oak_wood_planks`. CC0 licensing verified at https://polyhaven.com/license.
- Human visitor and animations: Quaternius Universal Animation Library, CC0 verified at https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library; glTF distribution at https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library. Converted the glTF and its buffer into a self-contained GLB. Uses `Walk_Loop`, `Idle_Loop`, `Interact`.
- Landscape tree: Kenney Nature Kit `tree_default.glb`, CC0 verified at https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit.

Downloaded media is served through Lovable Assets pointers. Buildings and abstract sculptures are original code-modelled geometry with architectural slabs, glazing, mullions, thresholds, timber doors and shared paths. No suitable licensed model of Louisiana was found.
